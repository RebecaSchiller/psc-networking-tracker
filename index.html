How it works

Up and running in minutes.

Network Momentum connects to a Google Sheet you control. Your data never touches our servers.

1

Create a Google Sheet

Go to sheets.google.com, create a blank spreadsheet, and rename the first tab to Contacts.

2

Add the Apps Script

Paste a small script that bridges the app and your sheet. Full instructions are inside the app.

3

Connect and go

Paste your Web App URL. Add your Anthropic API key to unlock AI suggestions.

4

Start tracking

Add your first contact, set a weekly goal, and let the app help you stay consistent.
